Notification & Communication Consent Policy
Last Updated: July 5, 2026

This Notification & Communication Consent Policy explains how Specialist Connect Pro uses various communication channels to send you important updates and how you can control your preferences.

1. Channels We Use
We may use the following channels to communicate with you depending on your account activity and preferences:

- Push Notifications: Real-time alerts sent directly to your mobile device through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). Used for referral updates, subscription alerts, and account activity.
- Email: Transactional emails for referral notifications, account updates, subscription confirmations, and support replies.
- SMS: Text messages for referral alerts and account-related updates delivered to your registered mobile number.
- WhatsApp: WhatsApp messages for referral and subscription notifications delivered through Twilio's WhatsApp Business API.
- In-App Notifications: Messages visible inside the app's notification timeline. These remain available regardless of other channel settings.
- OTP / Authentication Messages: One-time passwords and verification codes sent via SMS or Firebase phone authentication to secure your account. These are required for login and account security.

2. Consent and Opt-In
By registering for and using Specialist Connect Pro, you acknowledge and consent to receiving:

- Transactional and service-related communications necessary for platform operation (account verification, referral updates, subscription management, support responses);
- OTP/authentication messages required for secure login and account access;
- Push notification alerts if you have granted permission on your device.

You may provide separate consent for SMS, WhatsApp, and email marketing communications if such features are introduced in the future.

3. Managing Your Preferences
You can control which notification channels you receive at any time:

- In the Mobile App: Go to Profile > Notification Preferences to toggle Push, Email, SMS, and WhatsApp on or off individually.
- On Your Device: Push notification permissions can be managed through your device's system settings.
- Note: In-app notifications and OTP/authentication messages cannot be disabled as they are essential for platform functionality.

4. Opt-Out
You may opt out of non-essential communication channels (Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Push) at any time through your Notification Preferences in the app. Opting out of these channels will not affect your ability to use the platform, but you may miss certain real-time updates.

To opt out of all non-essential communications or request account deletion, contact us at specialistconnetpro@gmail.com.

5. Data Used for Notifications
To deliver notifications, we use the following data:

- Your registered mobile number (for SMS, WhatsApp, and OTP);
- Your email address (for email notifications);
- Your device's FCM push token (for push notifications, stored securely in our database);
- Your notification preferences (stored in your account settings).

We do not share your contact details with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Third-Party Service Providers
We use the following third-party services to deliver communications:

- Twilio: SMS and WhatsApp message delivery (Twilio Privacy Policy);
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): Push notification delivery (Google Privacy Policy);
- Email delivery services as configured by the platform administrator.

These providers process your data only for the purpose of delivering the requested communications.

7. Data Retention
We retain your notification preferences, device tokens, and communication logs for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, device tokens are removed and communication logs are anonymized.

8. Your Rights
You have the right to:

- Access the communication data we hold about you;
- Update your contact details and notification preferences;
- Request deletion of your account and associated communication data;
- Lodge a complaint with applicable data protection authorities.

To exercise these rights, contact us at specialistconnetpro@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Notification & Communication Consent Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the platform after changes take effect indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
