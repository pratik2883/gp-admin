<h1>Delete Account – Specialist Connect PRO</h1>

<p>Last Updated: June 7, 2026</p>

<p>
At Specialist Connect PRO, users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.
This page explains how to request account deletion, what information may be deleted, and what information may be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons.
</p>

<h2>How to request account deletion</h2>
<p>
To request deletion of your Specialist Connect PRO account, please send an email to
<a href="mailto:specialistconnetpro@gmail.com">specialistconnetpro@gmail.com</a>
from your registered email address.
</p>

<p>Use the subject line: <strong>Account Deletion Request</strong></p>

<p>Please include the following details in your email:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Your full name</li>
  <li>Your registered mobile number</li>
  <li>Your registered email address</li>
  <li>Your role or profile type (if applicable)</li>
  <li>Any additional details that help us identify your account</li>
</ul>

<h2>What happens after your request</h2>
<p>
After we receive your request, we will verify your identity and review the request.
Once verified, we will process the deletion request within a reasonable period, typically within 7 to 30 business days.
</p>

<h2>What data may be deleted</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Account profile information</li>
  <li>Login and registration details</li>
  <li>Support requests associated with your account</li>
  <li>Uploaded files, attachments, or reports, where deletion is permitted</li>
  <li>Other personal information associated with normal platform usage</li>
</ul>

<h2>What data may be retained</h2>
<p>
Certain information may be retained where necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution,
security, recordkeeping, backup recovery, or enforcement of our terms and policies.
We may also retain limited technical or transactional records where required by law or legitimate business purposes.
</p>

<h2>Delete data without deleting account</h2>
<p>
If you want to request deletion of specific personal data without deleting your full account,
you may also contact us at
<a href="mailto:specialistconnetpro@gmail.com">specialistconnetpro@gmail.com</a>
with the subject line <strong>Data Deletion Request</strong>.
</p>
